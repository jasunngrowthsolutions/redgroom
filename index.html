Complete Day-by-Day Expedition

Early morning departure from Delhi / Gurugram in a comfortable private car or luxury traveller. Climb along the scenic banks of the Beas River, enjoying dramatic views of towering pine slopes. Arrive in Manali by afternoon/evening, check in to your verified hotel, and spend a relaxing evening strolling along the vibrant Mall Road.
Visit the ancient 16th-century Hadimba Devi Temple sheltered among giant deodar pines. Head into rustic Old Manali, visit Vashisht natural hot springs, and hike along scenic trails leading towards Jogini Waterfall. End the day with café-hopping and authentic local mountain cuisine.
Head to Solang Valley for panoramic Himalayan snow vistas and optional adventure activities. Cross the historic engineering feat of the Atal Tunnel (Rohtang) into the dramatic, high-altitude Lahaul Valley. Explore tranquil Sissu Village and marvel at Sissu Waterfall before returning to Manali for dinner and overnight stay.
Enjoy breakfast and check out from your hotel. Head downstream into Kullu Valley for thrilling river rafting on the Beas or optional paragliding (subject to weather & seasonal clearance). Begin the return road journey, arriving back in Delhi / Gurugram by late night.

Verified Tour Inclusions

  • Comfortable roundtrip transportation from Delhi / Gurugram.
  • 3 Nights accommodation in Manali in verified properties.
  • Daily breakfast and dinner (as per agreed meal plan).
  • All road tolls, driver allowances, parking, and state permits.
  • Dedicated local coordination support throughout the tour.

Traveler Guidelines

  • • Carry valid government photo IDs (Aadhaar / Passport).
  • • Pack adequate warm layers, windbreakers, and sturdy shoes.
  • • Atal Tunnel / Sissu access depends on administrative clearance.
  • • Adventure activities (rafting, paragliding) are optional.
  • • Please confirm customized room categories before final booking.
