The Nepal Collection

Nepal, the beautiful

Temples, lakes, Himalayan trails and living culture. Four distinct ways to experience the world's most soulful mountain nation.

Best time to visit

October–November and March–April offer clear skies and mild weather. Mustang is best from June–September.

Visa & entry

Indians need no visa. Others can obtain a visa-on-arrival at Kathmandu airport (USD 30 for 15 days).

Currency & connectivity

Nepalese Rupee (NPR). Indian ₹500/₹2000 notes are widely accepted. Local SIMs available at the airport.

Our story

About Redgroom

Founded in 2018 in Gurugram, Redgroom Journeys began as a small team obsessed with the idea that travel should feel personal, considered and effortless.

Today we design journeys across Nepal, India and a growing list of international destinations — but our founding principle hasn't changed: every trip is planned by a real human who has walked the route, stayed in the hotels and eaten at the restaurants we recommend.

We don't believe in cookie-cutter packages. Every itinerary on this site is a starting point — a template we refine with you over a call, adjusting pace, budget and interests until it feels exactly right.
